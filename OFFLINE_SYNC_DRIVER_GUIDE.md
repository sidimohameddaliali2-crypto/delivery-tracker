# Offline Delivery Completion - Driver Guide

## Overview

Your deliveries are now **automatically saved offline** when you're in buildings without internet connection. When you regain connectivity (at your bike or elsewhere), your completions automatically sync with the server.

## How It Works

### Visual Status Indicators

Each delivery shows a sync status indicator:

- **⏳ Pending sync** (Yellow) - Your completion was saved offline, waiting to sync
- **🔄 Syncing...** (Blue) - Currently uploading to server
- **✅ Synced** (Green) - Successfully saved to server
- **❌ Failed** (Red) - Sync failed, needs manual retry

### Delivery Completion Workflow

1. **Complete delivery while offline** - Take photo, confirm bags
   - Your completion is **instantly saved** on your phone
   - You see a sync status indicator on the delivery card

2. **Regain connectivity** - Walk to your bike or move to area with signal
   - Automatic sync starts within 2 seconds
   - Deliveries move from "pending" → "syncing" → "synced"

3. **No action needed** - Your phone continues syncing in background
   - System automatically retries failed syncs
   - Maximum 10 retry attempts per delivery

### Sync Status Summary

At the top of the deliveries list, you'll see a summary:
```
🔄 Syncing offline deliveries...
2 pending, 1 syncing, 1 failed
```

This tells you:
- How many completions are waiting to sync
- How many are currently syncing
- How many failed and need attention

## What Happens If Sync Fails?

### Automatic Retry

The system automatically retries failed syncs with increasing delays:
- **1st attempt**: Immediate
- **2nd attempt**: After 1 second
- **3rd attempt**: After 5 seconds
- **4th attempt**: After 30 seconds
- **5th attempt**: After 5 minutes
- Continues up to 10 attempts

### Manual Retry

If a delivery shows **"❌ Failed"** status:

1. Check your internet connection
2. Click the **"Retry Sync"** button on that delivery card
3. System will attempt to sync immediately

## Troubleshooting

### "Pending sync" stuck for a while?

1. Check your internet connection strength
2. Walk closer to a window or move to area with better signal
3. Click "Retry Sync" button if available
4. Check the sync status at the top - if it says syncing, wait a moment

### "Failed" status won't clear?

1. Check the error message displayed (truncated after "Failed:")
2. Common issues:
   - **Photo too large** - Take smaller/lower quality photos next time
   - **Network timeout** - Move to stronger signal area and retry
   - **Server error** - Contact support if it persists

### Lost internet for extended time?

Don't worry! Your completions are saved on your phone. When you reconnect:
1. Automatic sync will start
2. System will retry failed items automatically
3. All your offline work will eventually sync

## Important Notes

✅ **Your offline completions are safe** - Saved on your phone even if you turn it off
✅ **Automatic handling** - You don't need to do anything, system handles retries
✅ **Progress visible** - Always see what's syncing and what's succeeded
✅ **Manual control** - Can retry failed syncs whenever you want

## Best Practices

1. **Complete deliveries normally** - Don't wait for connectivity
2. **Monitor sync status** - Glance at indicators to confirm syncing
3. **Stay near signal when possible** - Helps sync complete faster
4. **Check status at end of day** - Make sure all deliveries synced before leaving

## Questions?

If you see unusual sync issues:
1. Note the exact error message
2. Take a screenshot of the sync status
3. Report to your manager/dispatcher with details

---

**Remember**: Your phone automatically saves and syncs your work. The sync status indicators just let you see what's happening behind the scenes.
